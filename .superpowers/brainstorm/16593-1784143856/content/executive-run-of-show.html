What executives should remember

One connected operating model: less intake friction, better information before the visit, measurable workflow outcomes, and a governed validation path.

27–30 min

Discussion and decision

Choose validation partners, approve a demo-hardening sprint, and identify the first operational pilot.

Stable by design

  • Historian and SDNE are the only fully live moments.
  • Each live moment has a 60–90 second recorded fallback.
  • Triage, Scribe, and Clara use controlled outputs or short clips.

Guardrails

  • Use synthetic data only.
  • Do not call POCs clinically validated.
  • Keep Clara framed as an adjacent operator capability.
