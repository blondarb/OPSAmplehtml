Proposed slide sequence

Ten audience-facing slides. The slides carry the argument; the demonstrations provide the evidence.

1 · OPEN

Outpatient neurology can become one connected operating system

A connected path from referral to clinician readiness.

2 · THE OPERATING PROBLEM

The real cost today is fragmentation between referral, intake, exam, and documentation

Show the repeated handoffs, missing context, duplicate questions, and clinician preparation burden—without inventing ROI figures.

3 · THE PATIENT JOURNEY

One synthetic referral will show how the handoffs disappear

Introduce one fictional outpatient neurology case and carry it through the rest of the session.

4 · CONTROLLED DEMO

Triage turns an unstructured referral into an actionable care path

Show urgency, red flags, routing, and the bounded context handed forward.

Precomputed output or short clip
5 · LIVE DEMO

The Historian collects missing context before the clinician enters the room

Let executives hear a short, natural exchange and see the structured result.

Live anchor #1
6 · LIVE DEMO

SDNE adds objective exam data to the pre-visit picture

Use the headset for the memorable physical moment, then show the returned finding.

Live anchor #2
7 · CONTROLLED DEMO

The Scribe turns the accumulated evidence into clinician-ready documentation

Show a source-labeled preparation/report view, while clearly labeling any handoff not yet fully proven.

Precomputed output or short clip
8 · ADJACENT CAPABILITY

Clara applies the same orchestration pattern to inbound neurologic calls

Frame Clara as a physician-to-physician OneCall capability, not as a step in this outpatient journey.

Controlled demo
9 · EVIDENCE PLAN

The next phase is measurement, not broader claims

Pilot measures: referral-processing time, routing time, overrides, intake completion, duplicate questions, and clinician preparation time.

10 · DECISION

Approve one demo-hardening sprint and one governed pilot

Name the validation partners, lock the integrated demo, and choose the first operational workflow to measure.

Communication job

By the end, executives and administrators should support a focused demo-hardening and validation plan because the technology is most valuable as a connected outpatient operating model—not as five isolated AI experiments.
